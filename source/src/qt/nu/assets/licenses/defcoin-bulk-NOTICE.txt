Defcoin Bulk Paper Wallet Design Notice
=======================================

Nu Paper Wallet Design 5 uses the front and back artwork and paper-wallet
layout model from sibios/defcoin-bulk:

    https://github.com/sibios/defcoin-bulk

The upstream index.htm describes the software lineage as a fork of
bitaddress.org / CryptoLife Universal Paper Wallet Generator and includes the
MIT license text for the bitaddress.org software. The upstream header also
states that the artwork is licensed under the Creative Commons Freeworks
license:

    https://creativecommons.org/freeworks

Nu uses Core-native key generation and QR rendering. It does not import the
2014 browser cryptography stack from defcoin-bulk.
